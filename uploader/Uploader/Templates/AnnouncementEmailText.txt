Something I've wanted to build for a long time is finally live: every design in the catalog can now be opened directly in the pattern editor.

Until now, the editor was really for one thing — turning your own photo into a brand-new pattern. But quite a few of you asked for a way to open a design you found on the site the same way, so you could tweak it before you stitch: swap a color that doesn't quite match your floss stash, adjust the size, hide colors while you work, or just make it a little more yours.

Now you can. Just open any design's page and look for the new button: "Open this pattern in the editor." One click, and the whole chart is right there, fully editable.
