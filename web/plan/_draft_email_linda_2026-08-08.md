One quick thing that might help: the finished size of a cross-stitch
piece isn't fixed by the pattern alone — it depends on the fabric you
stitch it on. The same design comes out bigger on a coarser fabric
(11-count) and smaller on a finer one (18-count). Most of my patterns
are sized against standard 14-count Aida, which is the most common
choice, so that's what the sizes below assume — but if a design is close
to what you want and not exact, changing the fabric count can get you
right to 5x7 or 8x10 without changing the pattern at all.

Here's what I'd suggest for each:

🐘 Elephant — close to 8x10:
https://cross-stitch.com/Elephant-37-304-Free-Design.aspx (about 8.2" x 10.4" on 14-count)

🦊 Fox — close to 8x10:
https://cross-stitch.com/Red-Fox-37-212-Free-Design.aspx (about 7.9" x 10" on 14-count)

🦒 Giraffe — close to 5x7:
https://cross-stitch.com/Giraffe-54-404-Free-Design.aspx (about 5.1" x 7.1" on 14-count)

🦌 Fawn — this one's trickier. I don't have a fawn design that naturally
lands at 5x7 or 8x10 the way the others do — my closest is a square
design (about 10" x 10" on 14-count):
https://cross-stitch.com/Baby-Fawn-37-138-Free-Design.aspx
If you'd like, I can also look into adding a fawn pattern sized closer
