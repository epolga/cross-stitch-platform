Dear Geetha,

Thank you so much for sharing these — you asked for my frank views, so
here they are: I'm genuinely impressed.

That flower garland for your daughter-in-law's window is beautiful — the
mix of colors along the vine is so cheerful, and I can only imagine how
many individual flowers went into it. The little cricket-kit ball for

that gets used, not just admired.

real skill, not just following a pattern. I'm so sorry the woman who
first asked for it didn't want to pay for all that work after everything
you put in. But I love how it turned out in the end — your daughter-in-
law was right to claim her! The black hair, the bangles, the little
sari-style skirt — she's completely herself now, not just "the doll
someone didn't want." That's a lovely story, even with the frustrating
part at the start.

The coin purse and the scrunchie are so pretty too — that owl-ish purse
shape with the little flower button is clever. And that bunny with the
heart is precious — whoever your son's friend's baby is, they're a lucky
little one.

You mentioned you've made a kangaroo with a joey in its pouch — now
you've got me curious, I'd love to see that one if you ever have a
photo!

Thank you again for trusting me with these. It's lovely to see what your
hands can do outside of thread and fabric too.
