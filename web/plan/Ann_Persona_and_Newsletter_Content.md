# Ann Logan — persona backstory & recurring newsletter content ideas

Working reference for periodic "Ann" emails (site owner persona used in customer-facing
correspondence, e.g. thank-you replies, newsletters). Not published anywhere — internal
continuity document so future emails don't contradict each other.

## Established facts (already public on the site)

- Name: **Ann Logan**
- Location: Prague, Czech Republic (site footer address: Krivoklatska 271, Praha, 19900)
- Contact: ann@cross-stitch.com
- Site bio line (public): "Built by a cross-stitch designer who has spent more hours than
  she'd like to admit counting stitches from a paper chart."
- Site favicon/avatar: a black circle with "N" — canonically Ann's cat, **Nitka**.

## New facts (2026-07-08, confirmed by Olga — not yet public, no prior contradicting mentions)

- **Age: 60.**
- **Mild arthritis in her hands**, roughly 5-7 years' standing. Not disabling, but long
  stitching sessions with a paper chart became painful — squinting at small print, holding
  pages flat, losing her place in the grid. This is the real origin story for the site: it
  started as a personal tool so she could keep stitching comfortably (large adjustable
  screen, no need to hold paper, easy to pause and resume, hide-colors-while-stitching
  feature grew directly out of this need).

## Full backstory

- Learned to stitch as a child from her grandmother, **Milena** — inherited an old, mostly
  unlabeled stash of DMC floss from her that Ann still works through.
- Spent most of her career as a primary-school art teacher; cross-stitch was her personal
  outlet, not her job.
- Husband: **Tomáš**, retired engineer. Doesn't stitch, but built her first tablet stand for
  the hoop.
- Daughter: **Klára**, around 30, lives independently. Learned to stitch from Ann as a
  teenager; occasionally sends Ann photos of her own finished pieces.
- Cat: **Nitka** ("little thread" in Czech) — elderly, unbothered, has a talent for sleeping
  on exactly the part of the fabric Ann needs next.

## Recurring story threads for periodic emails

1. **"My arthritis and me"** — honest but light notes on good/bad stitching days, small
   coping tricks (fingerless gloves, warm water before starting, short sessions). Meant to
   resonate with the (likely large) share of the audience with similar hand issues — this is
   relatable, not a sob story.
2. **The eternal WIP** — one personal project Ann has been stitching for years, in short
   sessions because of her hands. Recurring "barely any closer to finished" updates, played
   for warmth/humor.
3. **Milena's box** — the unlabeled inherited floss stash; occasional "which DMC number is
   this?" detective stories — doubles as genuinely useful color-matching content.
4. **Klára updates** — occasional warm notes about her daughter's own stitching.
5. **Nitka mishaps** — light recurring humor: cat sits on the one section of fabric currently
   needed, steals scissors, naps on an unfinished piece, etc.
